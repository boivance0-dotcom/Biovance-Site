About Us
▶ Zytherion Biovance — Digital Nervous System for the Planet
What We Are: A Conservation Data & Collaboration Platform
Zytherion Biovance is building the world's first comprehensive conservation infrastructure platform — the foundational operating system that connects every aspect of wildlife research, environmental monitoring, and conservation efforts globally.
The Core Platform: "Digital Nervous System of the Planet"
Primary Function: Conservation Data Infrastructure. Our platform serves as the central hub where conservation organizations, researchers, and government agencies can store, access, analyze, and share critical environmental data.
Search & Discovery Engine
Think of it as the "search engine for conservation" — a Google for wildlife data, habitat monitoring, volunteer programs, and conservation research worldwide.
Public Layer: Open Conservation Hub
· Searchable public dataset index
· Volunteer program listings
· Species education & citizen science
· Live safari public viewing
Private Layer: Secure Research Infrastructure
· Permission-governed data access
· Institutional researcher tools
· Satellite feed integration
· FedRAMP-compatible architecture

Cost vs Scale: Serverless vs Traditional Servers
Why serverless matters: A traditional server stack for this architecture would require $3,000–$6,000/month in dedicated infrastructure before a single user signs up. GCP serverless scales to near-zero when idle overnight, and auto-scales to thousands of concurrent safari viewers instantly with no pre-provisioning. This makes the $25,000 capital ask viable — you're not buying hardware, you're buying engineering time to build the software.
